Description:
Abstract:
This dataset contains a select subset of Argos and GPS locations collected by satellite data collection systems from collared adult female polar bears that were instrumented in the southern Beaufort Sea between 1985-2015.
Purpose:
These data were collected to gain insights into movements of southern Beaufort Sea polar bears. These data were collected from adult female polar bears who had dependent young at the time of their collar application and whose satellite-linked transmitters transmitted location data for at least 300 days.

Process_Step:
Process_Description:
METHODS - FIELD:
We programmed Argos transmitters with a duty cycle of 4-12 hours every 1-7 days to transmit digitally encoded messages that could be received by the Argos System (Argos, www.argos-system.org). We programmed collars that estimated locations onboard with the use of a GPS to process a location estimate every 1-8 hours, which were then retained in a memory bank onboard the collar and transmitted with a duty cycle of 4 hours every 1-2 days to the Argos data collection system as a digitally encoded message.
We collected Argos satellite telemetry locations from a sample of 40 adult female polar bears between 6 May 1985 and 26 March 2012.
We collected GPS satellite telemetry locations from a sample of 22 adult female polar bears between 5 April 2004 and 31 March 2015.
Process_Date: Unknown
Process_Step:
Process_Description:
METHODS - ANALYSIS:
We used the Douglas Argos-filter (Douglas et al. 2012) with the 'hybrid' option to filter Argos locations with an algorithm based on spatial redundancy, movement rates, and turning angles with a maximum rate of 10 km/h and rejected locations at the apex of highly acute angles (RATECOEF = 15). We selected the location that was the highest quality per duty-cycle. GPS locations were not filtered.
We selected data from adult female polar bears who had dependent young at the time of their collar application and whose satellite-linked transmitters transmitted location data for at least 300 days.
